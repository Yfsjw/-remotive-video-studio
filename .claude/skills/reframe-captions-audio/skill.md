# Reframe, Captions & Audio Skill

## Reframing
Produce a true 1080x1920 frame without black padding. Center crop is acceptable as a fallback; speaker-aware reframing is preferred when reliable face/subject evidence exists.

## Captions
- Burn in readable captions.
- Keep lines short.
- Preserve timing from transcript.
- Never hide critical visuals.

## Audio
- Preserve speech intelligibility.
- Avoid unexplained long silences.
- Music, when present, remains subordinate to speech.
